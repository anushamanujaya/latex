# Phase B accounting decisions requiring owner sign-off

Status: **OPEN — no new accounting policy approved** (2026-11-09). This is a decision register, not an authorization to post entries. The implemented safe boundaries are documented in [the cross-ledger policy audit](PHASE_B_CROSS_LEDGER_POLICY.md). Until the finance owner signs a row, the application must continue to reject the corresponding unsupported correction. Historical posted records, dispatch boundaries, and approvals must remain intact.

| Case | Existing treatment | Finance-owner decision required before expansion | Ledgers / records at risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fully cash-paid purchase, same supplier and same cash account, undispatched | Independently approved void/replacement preserves the original payment and posts only the signed cash difference. Operator must confirm that additional cash or an actual supplier refund occurred. | Required proof/reference and timing of the real cash movement; who can certify receipt/refund; treatment when the difference cannot be physically collected or returned. | Cash movement and shift, supplier payable, purchase replacement, audit. |
| Partially or fully settled via separately allocated supplier payment | Replacement is blocked while a posted supplier payment is allocated. The payment can be independently reversed through its own compensating workflow first. | Whether partial deallocation, credit carry-forward, and reallocation are permitted; evidence and ordering of bank refund/repayment; treatment of a closed cashier period. | Supplier payment allocation, supplier payable, cash/bank, shift, purchase cost. |
| Cash-paid purchase changing supplier or cash account | Blocked; already-paid funds are not silently transferred. | Whether the original supplier must refund, whether a documented inter-supplier credit is lawful, how a cross-account transfer is approved, and when the replacement may be posted. | Two supplier ledgers, one or two cash/bank accounts, shifts, replacement purchase. |
| Supplier advance recovery or repayment correction | Eligible unused issuance, purchase allocation, and repayment reversals use recorded compensating entries and independent approvals. | How to handle partially consumed advances when a purchase is corrected; whether recovery moves to a replacement, is refunded, or remains an asset; treatment of advances lacking original account provenance. | Advance asset, supplier payable, purchase allocation, cash/bank, audit. |
| Purchase included in a confirmed dispatch, including an invoiced batch | Same-supplier correction with unchanged settlement may post an approved cost/quantity delta. Original purchase, dispatch membership/boundary, invoice revenue, and receipts remain historical. | Rules for changed supplier, cash, payment, or advance; whether buyer notification/re-invoicing is required for corrected cost/weight; treatment of a lower cost than already settled. | Supplier and cash/bank ledgers, dispatch inventory/profit, buyer invoice/receivable, audit. |
| Company accepted-KG or selling-rate amendment after invoice/receipt | Approved reconciliation posts linked invoice debit/credit and buyer ledger entry. Excess receipts are tracked as refund due and paid only through a separate approved refund. | Required weighbridge evidence/retention, buyer notification/credit-note wording, tax/discount classification, and recovery procedure before reversing an already-refunded receipt. | Dispatch revenue/profit, invoice, buyer receivable/refund due, cash/bank. |
| Cash or bank adjustment, overdraft, backdated posting | Ordinary debits cannot overdraw cash; nonzero bank overdraft requires independent approval. Transfers/reversals retain compensating history. | Authorized overdraft limits and expiry, evidence for opening-balance and legacy negative-balance corrections, backdating/cutoff treatment, cheque clearing and reconciliation policy. | Account movement ledger, cashier shift, bank statement, audit. |

For each approved decision, record the finance owner, approval date, effective date, evidence/reference fields, exact debit/credit mapping, handling of partially settled cases, role limits, period-close rule, and a worked example. Engineering can then implement and test the specific workflow on a separate synthetic database. No row above authorizes an automatic migration or historical rewrite.
